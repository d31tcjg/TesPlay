# Navigation

The bottom dock opens **Home**, **Live TV**, **Movies**, **Series**, **YouTube** and **My List**. Home combines previews from connected sources; Movies, Series and Live TV can filter by connection.

The upper-right profile button switches profiles and opens account controls. Expand the adjacent arrow for History, Search, Connections and Settings. Phone browsers also show the remote-control shortcut. Narrow desktop or tablet windows do not become phone browsers merely because they are small.

## Settings

Settings shows one category at a time: Account, Playback, Content, Browsers, Connections, Profiles and owner-only Household accounts. Account includes sign-out, phone pairing and fullscreen access. TesPlay has no paid-plan or billing section.

## Phone remote

Pair from **Settings → Account → Pair a phone with this screen**. On the phone, Browse and Queue remain available while a sliding panel exposes playback controls. Close the panel, press its button again, tap outside it or use Escape to return to browsing.

The phone and screen must reach the same installation. Account-authorized phones can reconnect to an available screen; guest remotes require pairing. See [the user guide](usage.md) for QR login and rear-screen behavior.

## Keyboard and motion

Navigation buttons support keyboard focus. Escape closes expanded quick actions and returns focus to their toggle. Clicking or focusing outside closes the expanded controls. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
